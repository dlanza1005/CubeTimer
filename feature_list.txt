CubeTimer — Features
====================
Timer
-Press SPACE to begin inspection countdown (15s)
-Press SPACE again to start the solve timer
-Press SPACE again to stop and record the solve
-Display last solve time when idle

Data
-Upon timer stop, create a solve datapoint containing [solveStartDateTimestamp, solveDuration, comment, boolDeleted] and append to Session Data.
-Upon clicking Save, session data is appended to data.txt on disk
-Delete a solve by clicking its point on the graph [boolDeleted=TRUE]

Graph
-Scatterplot of all session times
-Rolling average-of-5 (ao5) line overlay
-Std deviation boxes per 20-solve window
-Hover over a point to see its time
-Hover over ao5/a10 to see stats
-Resizable window, graph fills available space
